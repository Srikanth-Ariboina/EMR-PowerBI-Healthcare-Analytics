EMR POWER BI SAMPLE PROJECT
============================

Recommended model: Star schema

DIMENSIONS
- DateDim[Date]
- Patients[PatientID]
- Doctors[DoctorID]
- Departments[DepartmentID]

FACT TABLES
- Encounters
- Admissions
- LabOrders
- Pharmacy
- Billing

Suggested relationships:
DateDim[Date] 1:* Encounters[EncounterDate]
DateDim[Date] 1:* Admissions[AdmissionDate]
DateDim[Date] 1:* LabOrders[TestDate]
DateDim[Date] 1:* Pharmacy[SaleDate]
DateDim[Date] 1:* Billing[BillDate]

Patients[PatientID] 1:* Encounters[PatientID]
Patients[PatientID] 1:* Admissions[PatientID]
Patients[PatientID] 1:* LabOrders[PatientID]
Patients[PatientID] 1:* Pharmacy[PatientID]
Patients[PatientID] 1:* Billing[PatientID]

Doctors[DoctorID] 1:* Encounters[DoctorID]
Doctors[DoctorID] 1:* Admissions[DoctorID]
Doctors[DoctorID] 1:* LabOrders[DoctorID]
Doctors[DoctorID] 1:* Pharmacy[DoctorID]

CORE DAX
---------
Total Encounters = COUNTROWS(Encounters)

Completed Encounters =
CALCULATE([Total Encounters], Encounters[VisitStatus] = "Completed")

Total Patients = DISTINCTCOUNT(Encounters[PatientID])

Total Admissions = COUNTROWS(Admissions)

Average LOS = AVERAGE(Admissions[LOS_Days])

Total Bed Revenue = SUM(Admissions[BedRevenue])

Total Lab Revenue = SUM(LabOrders[NetAmount])

Total Pharmacy Revenue = SUM(Pharmacy[NetAmount])

Total Billing Revenue = SUM(Billing[NetBill])

Total Hospital Revenue =
[Total Bed Revenue] + [Total Lab Revenue] +
[Total Pharmacy Revenue] + [Total Billing Revenue]

Average Lab TAT = AVERAGE(LabOrders[TAT_Minutes])

Pending Lab Orders =
CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(LabOrders), LabOrders[ResultStatus] = "Pending")

Cancelled Encounters =
CALCULATE([Total Encounters], Encounters[VisitStatus] = "Cancelled")

Cancellation % =
DIVIDE([Cancelled Encounters], [Total Encounters], 0)

Revenue per Patient =
DIVIDE([Total Hospital Revenue], [Total Patients], 0)

Current Year Revenue =
CALCULATE([Total Hospital Revenue], YEAR(DateDim[Date]) = YEAR(TODAY()))

Monthly Revenue Trend:
Use DateDim[YearMonth] on X-axis and [Total Hospital Revenue] as Values.

DASHBOARD PAGES
---------------
1. Executive Overview
   KPI cards: Patients, Encounters, Admissions, Revenue, Avg LOS
   Charts: Monthly Revenue, Department Encounters, Revenue by Bill Type

2. Patient & OPD Analytics
   New/returning patient analysis, doctor performance, department volume

3. IPD / Admission Analytics
   Admissions, LOS, ward utilization, bed revenue, admission type

4. Laboratory Analytics
   Test volume, revenue, pending tests, average TAT, test-wise analysis

5. Pharmacy Analytics
   Medicine sales, quantity, revenue, payment status

6. Billing & Finance
   Gross bill, discount, net revenue, payment mode/status

INTERVIEW PROJECT STORY
-----------------------
"Built an EMR analytics dashboard using a star schema to monitor patient
volume, OPD/IPD activity, laboratory TAT, pharmacy revenue and billing.
Used Power Query for transformation, DAX for KPIs and time intelligence,
and Power BI visuals for management reporting."
